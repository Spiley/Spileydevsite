Exploding Booze — iPhone privacy policy

Updated 21 October 2026

Who operates the app

Spiley operates Exploding Booze. Contact spileyapps@gmail.com for support, inappropriate content reports or privacy requests. The iPhone edition has no advertising, advertising tracking or purchases.

One phone

Player names, optional camera photos, scores, finished stories, settings and translation caches are stored on this device. Local player photos are not uploaded by the iPhone app. You can play local games without an online profile. Camera access is optional and only requested for a local photo or room QR code; a room code can also be entered manually.

Multiple phones

Joining or creating a room uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Realtime Database. A random account ID, chosen name, room membership, connection timestamps, votes, scores, actions and player-written statements are processed to run and reconnect the game. Room participants see the relevant game content. Hidden roles and answers are restricted by database rules. iPhone and mixed rooms use fixed avatars and do not accept uploaded player photos. Keep room codes within your own group.

Service providers

Firebase is provided by Google and processes online data and technical network information to operate and secure the service. Apple Translation translates on this device and manages optional language downloads; Apple may process translation service usage and performance information. The app does not send game text to a cloud translation service or install Firebase Analytics or advertising SDKs on iPhone.

Retention and deletion

Room access expires after 24 hours. Expiry alone does not erase database records; expired rooms are removed through the maintenance process. Delete your data below to close known rooms and erase this phone's online identity and local app data. Older associated data is queued for removal; contact support if it has not been addressed within 30 days. Support reports and minimal abuse-prevention records are retained only as needed to investigate, protect players and meet legal obligations. Apple language downloads and operating-system or service-provider logs are managed by those providers.

Your choices and rights

You can omit a photo, use a nickname, play offline, revoke camera permission in iOS Settings, block other players and request access, correction or deletion via support. If applicable, you may complain to your local data protection authority. Other room participants may have seen or captured shared content; the app cannot erase their screenshots or memories.

Player conduct

Use rooms only with people you know. Do not post harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual content or anyone else's private information. Report and block inappropriate players in Room settings. Spiley can remove content, close rooms or restrict abusive online identities. Game penalties are score points; no alcohol is needed. Do not attempt unsafe or unwanted activities.

Contact Spiley support
